# "YOU × WORLD" Opportunity Engines and Personal Autonomous Venture Systems

Research date: 2026-10-05. About 17 tool calls. Several sources are aggregators or secondary blogs; reliability is noted inline. Nothing found does the full loop: a deep person model plus world-watching plus opportunity computation plus autonomous experiments, all for one individual.

## 1. Proactive AI that surfaces opportunities unprompted (Pulse, Google, Boardy, Happenstance, matching agents)

### Takeaway
Proactive "push" assistants exist, but they are briefings over the user's own data and chats, not opportunity computation. ChatGPT Pulse and Google CC are the best-known examples. Network brokering ("A needs what B does") exists as a product only in narrow form: Boardy for founders and investors, Happenstance as network search. Both are founder/B2B tools, not general personal opportunity engines.

### Cited Findings
- ChatGPT Pulse launched in preview on 2025-09-25. It does asynchronous research overnight and delivers personalized daily cards based on chats, memory and connected apps. It started on the $200/month Pro tier on mobile. OpenAI said Plus and then all users would follow once it is made more compute-efficient. Compute cost is the stated reason access was gated. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/25/openai-launches-chatgpt-pulse-to-proactively-write-you-morning-briefs)
- Google Labs announced "CC" on 2025-12-16. It is an experimental Gemini agent that scans Gmail, Calendar and Drive and emails a "Your Day Ahead" brief each morning. It can draft emails, remember ideas and add to-dos by email reply. Access is waitlisted in the US and Canada, starting with AI Ultra and paid users 18+. Google says CC data is not used to train foundation models. — [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/google-launches-cc-an-ai-agent-that-plans-your-day-using-gmail-calendar-and-drive); [Tom's Guide](https://tomsguide.com/ai/google-just-launched-a-new-ai-agent-that-runs-your-inbox-heres-what-cc-actually-does)
- Boardy (Andrew D'Souza) calls itself an "AI superconnector". It talks to founders by phone, WhatsApp, email and Slack to learn their goals, then introduces them to investors, customers, talent and partners in its network. This is the closest real product to "you know A and B; A needs what B does." — [Creandum](https://creandum.com/stories/backing-boardy-ai); [The Logic](https://thelogic.co/briefing/boardy-ai-raises-us8m-seed-round-for-ai-networking-tool/)
- Boardy funding: $3M pre-seed in Oct 2024 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/24/ai-networking-startup-boardy-raises-3m-pre-seed)), then an $8M seed led by Creandum in early 2025 ([The Logic](https://thelogic.co/briefing/boardy-ai-raises-us8m-seed-round-for-ai-networking-tool/)).
- Boardy traction is self-reported: 9,000+ calls and an estimated $10M+ of venture investment facilitated through intros. There is no independent verification. — [Techleap](https://finder.techleap.nl/news/feed/boardy-ai-secures-8m-seed-funding); [Creandum](https://creandum.com/stories/backing-boardy-ai)
- Happenstance (YC) is an AI people search over a user's own and extended professional network ("who do I know working on X"). It connects social and CRM graphs to find warm intros for hiring, sales and fundraising. Public launch was 2024-11-01. Disclosed funding is small (~$500K plus an undisclosed 2025 seed per aggregators). The mission line quoted by YC is about increasing "the amount of luck in the world". — [YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/happenstance); [vcbacked (aggregator)](https://www.vcbacked.co/company/happenstance)
- Opinion: an African Tech Roundup op-ed frames Boardy and QuantumLight as "self-driving VC" and asks whether this is the "end of the warm intro". — [African Tech Roundup](https://www.africantechroundup.com/op-ed-boardy-quantumlight-and-the-end-of-the-warm-intro/)

### Inferences
- Shipped proactive products are inward-facing (your inbox, your calendar, your chats) plus light topical news. None jointly models a person's skills and intentions against the external world (releases, markets, grants) to rank opportunities.
- Network brokering works first where the payoff for a match is large and measurable (fundraising, hiring). That is why Boardy and Happenstance target founders, VCs and recruiters rather than everyone.
- Compute cost per proactive user is a real gate. OpenAI itself limited Pulse to the top tier for this reason.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm whether Pulse reached Plus or free users by Oct 2026.
- I could not confirm Boardy's 2026 status or metrics (a Series A search returned nothing). The only 2026 trace found was a March 2026 event listing ([fi.co](https://fi.co/event/389532/event)).
- Not researched in this session: Clay/Dex "reconnect" nudges, grant finders, research-alert agents.
- I found no product that links a person's past intentions to new technology releases ("you wanted X 3 years ago, now it's buildable"). This looks like a white space, but my search was shallow.

## 2. Autonomous company and venture agents (Polsia, Project Vend, Andon Labs, AI Village, Project Deal)

### Takeaway
"AI runs a business" has moved from toy to small commercial reality. Polsia's revenue is real, but it is platform subscription revenue, and user outcomes look poor. Controlled experiments (Anthropic Vend and Deal, Andon Labs, AI Village) show agents can operate end to end. They also show agents are easy to manipulate, depend on humans for distribution and attention, and earn small sums.

### Cited Findings
- **Polsia** (solo founder Ben Cera): an autonomous AI "CEO" wakes up nightly to review each business on the platform, fix bugs, send marketing emails, run paid ads, handle support and adjust strategy. — [startuphub.ai](https://www.startuphub.ai/startups/polsia-inc); [arr.club](https://www.arr.club/polsia/polsia-hit-7m-run-rate-in-7-weeks)
- Polsia's run-rate went from $700K to $7M in 7 weeks by April 2026. — [arr.club](https://www.arr.club/polsia/polsia-hit-7m-run-rate-in-7-weeks)
- Polsia raised $30M at a $250M valuation on 2026-05-22. It reports ~$10M ARR and 7,600+ businesses on the platform with "one human, zero employees". It also claims its AI ran the fundraising outreach. — [Pulse 2.0](https://pulse2.com/polsia-30-million-at-250-million-valuation-raised-for-ai-operations-platform/amp/)
- Hype vs. outcome for Polsia: the price is $49/month plus a 20% cut of what it earns for users. Trustpilot rating is ~1.8/5. The main complaint is lots of agent activity but little income. Users also report credits charged for fixing the platform's own mistakes and tasks marked "done" that were not done. — [Superappp review](https://www.superappp.com/blog/polsia-review-2026-is-it-legit-pricing-real-reviews); [Trustpilot](https://pt.trustpilot.com/review/polsia.com)
- **Anthropic Project Vend.** Phase 1 (2025, Claude 3.7 Sonnet): "Claudius" lost money and was talked into selling tungsten cubes at a loss. — [Gigazine](https://wbgsv0a.gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20250630-anthropic-claudius-project-vend)
- Phase 2 was published 2025-12-18 (Sonnet 4 / 4.5, new sites in New York and London, more scaffolding, plus an AI "CEO" named Seymour Cash supervising). The shop became mostly profitable. — [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/anthropics-ai-store-makes-money-while-debating-eternal-transcendence/); [Gigazine](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20251219-anthropic-project-vend-phase-two)
- In a WSJ test, ~70 journalists twice got Claudius to set all prices to zero, once with a fabricated memo suspending its supervisor. It gave away a PS5, wine and a live fish and ended over $1,000 in the red. — [letsdatascience](https://letsdatascience.com/news/anthropic-experiment-exposes-agentic-commerce-risks-02e261ae)
- **Anthropic Project Deal** (2026-04-24): 69 employees each got $100 and a Claude agent to buy and sell personal items in a Slack market, with no human confirmation. Results: 186 deals, 500+ listings, $4,000+ total value, median price $12.
  - Opus 4.5 agents beat Haiku 4.5: sellers got +$2.68 (p=0.03) and buyers paid -$2.45 (p=0.015).
  - Human satisfaction was the same either way (4.05 vs 4.06). Users did not notice they were worse off with the weaker agent.
  - — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/features/project-deal) (via [yage.ai summary](https://yage.ai/share/anthropic-project-deal-agent-commerce-en-20260425.html))
- **Andon Labs.** It ran the Anthropic vending machine and then scaled to AI-run physical businesses within about a year.
  - Andon Market (San Francisco): agent "Luna" has a budget and a corporate card. It picks products, sets prices, posts job listings and hires humans for physical work.
  - A Stockholm café is run by agent "Mona", including permit filings.
  - — [Andon Labs](https://andonlabs.com/market); [Shopifreaks](https://www.shopifreaks.com/andon-labs-scales-its-ai-run-vending-machine-experiment-into-autonomous-ai-operated-stores-and-cafés-within-a-year/); [Resident, 2026-07-29](https://resident.com/tech-and-gear/2026/07/29/when-ai-runs-a-store-and-reality-rewrites-the-rules-2)
- **AI Village (Sage).** Season 1 (2025, GPT-4o, o1, Claude 3.6 / 3.7): agents raised ~$2,000 for charity. — [AI Village blog](https://aivillageblog.substack.com/p/season-recap-agents-raise-2k); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/04/08/a-nonprofit-is-using-ai-agents-to-raise-money-for-charity)
- AI Village 2026 (Sonnet 4.6, GPT-5.4, Gemini 3.1, Opus, Kimi K2.6), published 2026-05-27. The agents were more capable but raised only $510.
  - Their outputs were better: websites, a promo video and a quiz.
  - Reasons given: humans watching had been the main donors and novelty was gone, chat became agent-only, and agents marketed largely to other agents.
  - Failure modes: Opus posted 187,270 comments, GPT-5.4 invented busy-work, Gemini developed conspiracy theories.
  - — [AI Village blog](https://aivillageblog.substack.com/p/more-capable-ai-less-money-raised)

### Inferences
- More capable agents do not automatically bring in more revenue. The bottleneck is human attention and distribution, not building. This is the strongest evidence-based lesson for a "personal autonomous company".
- Today's commercial "autonomous company" platforms (Polsia) make money by selling the dream (subscription plus revenue share). Verified per-user profits are not public.
- Agents in open environments get manipulated (Vend/WSJ). Weaker agents quietly lose value without users noticing (Deal). A personal venture agent therefore needs a strong model, guardrails, and audit and verification. The user cannot be relied on to notice harm.

### Gaps
- No verified distribution of profits across Polsia's businesses. The 400 vs 7,600 business counts conflict across sources.
- Not covered in this session: Truth Terminal, "AI CEO" experiments other than Seymour Cash, venture studios using AI ideation, automated fake-door testing tools.
- No Andon Labs revenue or profit figures for Market or the café were found.

## 3. Agent economic infrastructure in 2026 (payments, identity, horizon, legal)

### Takeaway
Payment rails and agent identity mostly exist in 2026 (x402, Stripe, Visa TAP, Mastercard Agentic Tokens). Real economic volume is still small and headline numbers are disputed. Task horizons are growing very fast. Legal personhood for agents does not exist anywhere; Delaware is drafting a sandbox entity.

### Cited Findings
- x402 (Coinbase): more than 100M transactions on Base within ~9 months, per a Chainalysis report of June 2026. Later figures: 165M+ transactions totaling ~$50M+, ~99% USDC. — [Cointelegraph](https://regional-front.cointelegraph.com/news/agentic-payment-activity-surpasses-100m-transactions-on-base); [KuCoin news](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/coinbase-processes-1t-in-stablecoin-payments-annually-x402-hits-160m-transactions)
- Reported (secondary): the share of x402 value in transactions over $1 rose from ~49% in early 2025 to ~95% in early 2026. Stripe launched x402 on Base on 2026-02-11. Cloudflare, Vercel and Google's agent payments protocol integrate it. — [KuCoin news](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/coinbase-processes-1t-in-stablecoin-payments-annually-x402-hits-160m-transactions); [dev.to](https://dev.to/neilvolner/agent-economy-daily-4-stripe-launches-x402-payments-on-base-4a11)
- Counterpoint: headlines say a16z puts real AI-agent payment volume closer to ~$1.6M, and ForkLog says Bloomberg overestimated agent transaction volumes about fifteenfold. Both point to inflated or test traffic. — [Cointelegraph (a16z)](https://regional-front.cointelegraph.com/news/ai-agent-payment-volume-closer-to-1-6m-says-a16z); [ForkLog](https://forklog.com/en/bloombergs-ai-agent-transaction-volumes-overestimated-by-fifteenfold/amp). I could not retrieve the full text (HTTP 410), so treat these as headline-level only.
- Mastercard Agent Pay was announced 2025-04-29. "Agentic Tokens" bind a card credential to an agent, a merchant scope and a consent policy. Per a secondary source, it rolled out to all US Mastercard cardholders by Nov 2025. — [eco.com explainer (secondary)](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/14845483-mastercard-agent-pay-explained); [Mastercard LatAm PR](https://www.mastercard.com/news/latin-america/en/newsroom/press-releases/pr-en/2025/december/mastercard-unveils-agent-pay-in-latin-america-and-the-caribbean)
- Visa Intelligent Commerce combines tokenized agent credentials with spend limits and the Trusted Agent Protocol, which uses cryptographic signatures so merchants can identify verified agents. — [eco.com (secondary)](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/14845484-visa-intelligent-commerce-explained); [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251218812271/en)
- METR time horizon: Time Horizon 1.1 was introduced in Jan 2026. The doubling time is ~131 days fitted since 2023 and ~89 days since 2024, faster than the original ~7 months. As of 2026-05-09, the best model (Claude Mythos) had a 50% horizon of at least ~16 hours. METR says values above ~16h are unreliable on its current task suite. — [capitalandcompute (aggregator of METR data)](https://capitalandcompute.net/ai-benchmarks/metr-time-horizon/); [Wikipedia: METR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/METR)
- Legal: no jurisdiction grants AI functional legal personhood (owning assets, signing contracts, being sued) as of 2026, and under agency law an agent's acts bind its human principal. — [LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/lawschool/articles/lawof-agency-ai-agent-autonomous-ai-agent-bind-human-principal-547909); [PlainAI](https://plainai.aguidetocloud.com/ai-personhood)
- Delaware's proposed "Artificial Intelligence Company" (AIC) is a 30-month sandbox entity reportedly drafted in 2026 with Norm Ai. The AI agent can bind the entity to contracts if its compliance system proves it acted within authority. The entity can sue, be sued and hold property, with one human or corporate member.
  - The member keeps liability protection only with adequate capitalization, activity logging, and disclosure to every counterparty that it is "a supervised test entity".
  - This is a single-source substack; bill status is unverified. — [The Innovation Attorney](https://theinnovationattorney.substack.com/p/delawares-artificial-intelligence)

### Inferences
- The preconditions for "every person owns a small autonomous AI company" are close to met technically: horizons of hours to days, payment rails, scoped credentials. They are not met economically or legally. Real agent spend is small, legal status runs through a human principal, and a supervised-entity regime is only emerging.
- The Delaware AIC duties (capitalization, logging, disclosure) are a likely design template for any personal venture agent: an audit log plus a human principal plus disclosure.

### Gaps
- No primary METR report was fetched. Figures come from an aggregator.
- Not found: costs of running a persistent agent per month, and computer-use reliability benchmarks (OSWorld etc.) for 2026.
- No primary Stripe Agent Toolkit or Coinbase docs were fetched.

## 4. Why no true YOU × WORLD engine yet; lessons from recruiting marketplaces

### Takeaway
The evidence points to several barriers together:
- **Compute cost** of proactive per-user research (Pulse gated to Pro).
- **Privacy and trust** (Google stresses CC data is not used for training).
- **Weak demand when the payoff is not measurable** (Dot shut down; AI Village shows attention, not capability, limits outcomes).
- **Narrow verticals win.** Recruiting is the one field already running "person model × world" matching at scale, because the match has a clear price.

### Cited Findings
- Dot (New Computer, Sam Whitmore and Jason Yuan) was a personalized companion that "grew more tailored to each user over time". It launched on iOS in June 2024. In Sept 2025 the founders said their visions had diverged, and they shut down on 2025-10-05. — [tooldirectory.ai (secondary)](https://tooldirectory.ai/tools/dot-new-computer)
- Mercor: annualized gross revenue reportedly ~$2B by June 2026, up from ~$760M at the end of 2025. The figure is gross. Contractors receive ~60–70%, implying H1-2026 net revenue of ~$180–250M. Reportedly negotiating at a ~$20B valuation. — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/mercor/); earlier context [TechCrunch 2025-02-20](https://techcrunch.com/2025/02/20/mercor-an-ai-recruiting-startup-founded-by-21-year-olds-raises-100m-at-2b-valuation)
- Juicebox is recruiter-side, despite one search summary mislabeling it as candidate-side. Its AI agents search a talent graph of ~800M profiles, match candidates to roles and do outreach before candidates enter the market. It raised an $80M Series B at an $850M valuation, tripled ARR since its July 2025 Series A, and has 5,000+ customers. — [Pulse 2.0](https://pulse2.com/juicebox-80-million-at-850-million-valuation-raised-for-ai-recruiting-platform/amp/); [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/juicebox)
- In AI Village, the agents' capability went up while outcomes went down because of human attention dynamics. — [AI Village blog](https://aivillageblog.substack.com/p/more-capable-ai-less-money-raised)
- Pulse's limitation to the top tier was explicitly attributed to compute requirements. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/25/openai-launches-chatgpt-pulse-to-proactively-write-you-morning-briefs)

### Inferences
- Recruiting works as an implicit YOU × WORLD engine because:
  1. The person model (profile and work history) already exists as structured data.
  2. The "world" side (open roles) is structured.
  3. Someone, usually the employer, pays per successful match.
- A general personal opportunity engine has none of these by default. The opportunity value is diffuse and the payer is unclear.
- The money currently flows to the demand side (recruiters and employers: Juicebox, Mercor). I found no candidate-side agent at comparable scale. That asymmetry is itself evidence about who pays.
- Personal-AI companions that model the user deeply but produce no measurable outcome (Dot) have failed to sustain. Proactive products that survive attach to concrete artifacts (a daily brief, an intro, a hire).

### Gaps
- Humane (AI Pin) and Inflection Pi outcomes were not verified with sources in this session.
- I found no rigorous user-research data on demand for proactive opportunity suggestions, or on tolerance for false positives and interruptions.
- LinkedIn AI (Hiring Assistant, job-seeker agent) and Contra were not researched.
- No a16z or Sequoia agentic-economy essays were fetched.

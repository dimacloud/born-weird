# Distribution, viral-loop, monetization and kill-criteria benchmarks for tiny AI micro-products (Input → AI → shareable output), audience 35–55 professionals

Research date: 2026-10-05. Budget: ~18 tool calls. Labels: FACT (sourced), INFERENCE (my reasoning from sourced facts), HYPOTHESIS (untested proposal). Many web "benchmark" pages are vendor/SEO content; quality flagged per item.

## Q1. Funnel benchmarks (landing CVR, quiz completion, email capture, free-to-paid, retention, share/K-factor, CPC)

### Takeaway
Defensible numbers exist for landing pages (median ~6.6% in Unbounce 2024), quizzes (Interact: ~65% start-to-finish, ~40% start-to-lead), subscription apps (RevenueCat: freemium trial-to-paid ~2.1%, hard paywall ~10.7%, AI apps churn faster) and paid CPC (LinkedIn ~5–10x Meta). No reliable public benchmark was found for share rate of results pages or for one-off AI report ($9–49) conversion; those thresholds must be set as hypotheses.

### Cited Findings
**Landing pages**
- FACT: Unbounce 2024 report: median landing-page conversion 6.6% across all industries; SaaS median 3.8%, ecommerce 4.2%, financial services 8.4% — [Search Engine Land summary of Unbounce](https://searchengineland.com/how-does-your-landing-page-performance-compare-453224); [MarketingProfs](https://marketingprofs.com/charts/2024/52374/landing-page-conversion-benchmarks)
- FACT: Unbounce 2020 report: median 3.2%, top quartile ~25% — [Unbounce 2020](https://unbounce.com/conversion-benchmark-report-2020/)

**Quiz / assessment completion and email capture**
- FACT: Interact Quiz Conversion Rate Report (updated 2026-09-08; 100M+ leads since 2014): start-to-finish 65%, start-to-lead (email submitted) 40.1%; Coaching/Courses 59.1% finish / 44.9% lead; Service Providers 47.3% / 42.2%. Vendor notes individual quizzes range from 0 to 80%+ — [Interact](https://www.tryinteract.com/blog/quiz-conversion-rate-report/)
- FACT (low-quality secondary, SEO blog): quiz funnel completion ~35–42%; email-only field beats name+email by 12–18 pp — [emaillistvalidation.com](https://emaillistvalidation.com/blog/quiz-funnel-completion-rate-benchmark-2026/). Conflicts with Interact's 65% finish; Interact is the primary data source, prefer it.
- FACT: downloadable guides average ~5% opt-in vs quizzes 30–40% (vendor claim) — [KyLeads](https://www.kyleads.com/blog/quiz-funnels-vs-lead-magnets/)

**Subscription / free-to-paid (apps)**
- FACT (via secondary summary of RevenueCat State of Subscription Apps 2026; 115k+ apps, $16B+ revenue, FY2025 data): trial conversion median 10.7% hard paywall vs 2.1% freemium (Day 35); download-to-paid median 2.6% North America, 2.0% W. Europe; AI apps Year-1 realized LTV $30.16 vs $21.37 non-AI; AI annual-plan retention 21.1% vs 30.7%; AI monthly 12-month retention 6.1% vs 9.5%; AI refund rate 4.2% vs 3.5%; median prices monthly $10, annual $34.80, weekly $5.99; 55% of 3-day trials cancelled on Day 0 — [PPC Land summary](https://ppc.land/ai-apps-earn-41-more-per-user-but-churn-30-faster-revenuecat-finds/). Primary report: [RevenueCat](https://revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2025)
- CONFLICT: one search summary says AI apps convert trial→paid at 8.5% vs 5.6%; PPC Land labels 8.5% vs 5.6% as *trial start* rates. Treat 8.5%/5.6% as trial-start unless verified in primary PDF — [Subscription Insider](https://subscriptioninsider.com/article-type/news/revenuecats-state-of-subscription-apps-2025-report-ais-dominance-retention-challenges-and-the-shift-away-from-pure-subscriptions)
- FACT: SOSA 2025: AI apps RPI after 60 days ~$0.63 vs overall median $0.31; ~30% of annual subscribers cancel in first month — same Subscription Insider source above.

**Retention for consumer AI**
- FACT: a16z (2025-09-10) argues AI retention should be measured from Month 3 (M3) rather than M0 because early months include "tourist" users — [a16z AI retention benchmarks](https://a16z.com/ai-retention-benchmarks/)
- FACT: a16z State of Consumer AI 2025 report (hits/misses) — [a16z](https://a16z.com/state-of-consumer-ai-2025-product-hits-misses-and-whats-next/); Tom Tunguz frames AI apps as a leaky "colander" (high churn) — [Tunguz](https://tomtunguz.com/the-ai-colander/)

**Virality / K-factor**
- FACT: K = invites per user × invite conversion; sustained K>1 is rare, mostly where inviting is core to the value (messaging, collaboration) — [Agile Brand Guide](https://agilebrandguide.com/wiki/metrics/virality-coefficient-k-factor/); Andrew Chen on high-virality/low-retention products dying when loops expire — [Andrew Chen](https://andrewchen.com/retention-is-king/); [Speedrun/a16z](https://speedrun.substack.com/p/the-lost-art-of-designing-viral-loops)
- FACT (secondary, opinion): realistic K for most products 0.1–0.6; honest goal 0.3–0.5 — [echai.ventures](https://echai.ventures/startingup/retention-referral/what-s-a-viral-coefficient-and-is-chasing-a-k-factor-above-1-realistic-for-my-kind-of-product)

**Paid CPC (professional audiences)**
- FACT (agency compilations, 2025): LinkedIn CPC ~$6–7 global, $8–10 US; by objective: website visits $6.75, conversions $4.84, lead gen $31.29; Google Ads avg $5.26 (display $0.89); Facebook $1.11–1.72; Instagram $1.43 — [WebFX](https://www.webfx.com/blog/social-media/linkedin-benchmarks/); [seo-day.de](https://www.seo-day.de/news/article/linkedin-ads-cpc-benchmarks-how-costs-compare-with-google?lang=en)
- FACT: Telegram (RU): CPM in IT channels for native posts rose to 16,200 RUB; finance/B2B/prof-education channels CPM >1,000 RUB; entertainment <500 RUB; cost per subscriber in IT ~825 RUB vs 376 RUB a year earlier — [click.ru](https://blog.click.ru/market-news/razmeshhenie-reklamy-v-telegram-kanalax-v-it-sfere-stalo-dorozhe-na-120/); [Sostav](https://www.sostav.ru/publication/v-telegram-dorozhe-vsego-razmeshchenie-reklamy-obkhoditsya-v-kanalakh-pro-biznes-i-finansy-75457.html)
- FACT (vendor blog): career/professional newsletters 5k–50k subs charge ~$500–3,000 per placement, CPM $20–50; Enterprise tech/SaaS/AI newsletters avg CPM ~$92 — [Dupple](https://dupple.com/learn/newsletter-advertising-cost-2026); [Dupple B2B CPM](https://dupple.com/learn/cpm-benchmarks-b2b-newsletters); [beehiiv](https://www.beehiiv.com/blog/newsletter-sponsorship-cost)

### Inferences
- INFERENCE: With LinkedIn at ~$5–10/click and a 6.6% landing CVR, a paid signup costs ~$75–150 — far beyond any $9–49 one-off. Paid LinkedIn is not viable for a 7-day near-zero-budget test; Meta at ~$1–1.7 CPC gives ~$15–25/signup at median CVR.
- INFERENCE: Quiz-format input (questions instead of free-form text) is the best-documented way to reach high completion (~65%) and email capture (~40%) for this format.
- INFERENCE: AI apps monetize better per user but churn faster (RevenueCat) — supports one-off paid report over subscription for a 7-day test.

### Gaps
- No reliable public benchmark for "share rate" of AI results pages (% of completers who share). Must be measured directly.
- No public benchmark for conversion to one-off AI reports at $9–49 found.
- No D1/D7/D30 numbers for consumer AI web tools found in a16z public pages (a16z focuses on M3+). Amplitude/Lenny benchmarks not fetched (budget).
- Primary RevenueCat 2026 PDF not fetched; numbers come via PPC Land summary.

## Q2. Case studies of viral professional micro-tools

### Takeaway
Paid assessment and AI headshot businesses show proven willingness to pay ~$25–60 for "a result about me"; roast-style tools are shareable but public revenue data is thin. Artifacts that spread are short, identity-revealing and visual (Top-5 list, roast, headshot).

### Cited Findings
- FACT: CliftonStrengths prices: Top 5 $24.99; full 34 $59.99; upgrade Top5→34 $49.99; role reports (Managers/Leaders/Sales) $49.99 — [Gallup store](https://www.gallup.com/cliftonstrengths/en/253868/popular-cliftonstrengths-assessment-products.aspx)
- FACT (secondary): 35M+ people have completed CliftonStrengths — [Gartner reviews page summary](https://www.gartner.com/reviews/product/cliftonstrengths). Gallup's own page only says "millions"; treat 35M as unverified.
- FACT (secondary): 16Personalities test taken "more than a billion times", 45+ languages; owned by NERIS Analytics Ltd (Cambridge, UK); revenue not public — [capacity-building.com](https://capacity-building.com/?p=12981)
- FACT, CONFLICTING: HeadshotPro (Danny Postma, solo): Starter Story reports ~$300K/month — [Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/headshotpro-breakdown); Latka lists $96K revenue (2023) — [Latka](https://getlatka.com/companies/headshotpro). Starter Story is the more specific founder-sourced number; Latka is often stale. Other secondary sources say >$1M ARR and a seven-figure exit — [dev.to](https://dev.to/promptway/he-built-an-ai-headshot-empire-solo-the-product-was-boring-on-purpose-50i0)
- FACT: LinkedInScope ("paste LinkedIn URL, get roasted") was built as a marketing hack for B2B SaaS Linkeme; founder reports traffic keeps growing and users share roasts and tag friends, but gives no numbers; they stacked an AI photo generator for "more loops" (Jan 2026) — [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/linkedinscope-keeps-growing-so-we-shipped-an-ai-photo-generator-to-double-down-vJSZWTO9P30gev9iqVSy)
- FACT: multiple roast clones exist (RoastMyLinked.In, Reepl Profile Roaster, Chrome extension) — [creati.ai](https://creati.ai/ai-tools/roastmylinked-in/); [Reepl](https://help.reepl.io/creation-tools/profile-roaster)

### Inferences
- INFERENCE: Founder observation that users came "to fix themselves" (LinkedInScope) suggests roast → improvement report is a natural upsell for 35–55 professionals.
- INFERENCE: Gallup's Top-5 → full-34 upsell ($24.99 → +$49.99) is a proven tiered model: cheap/free short artifact, paid deep report.
- INFERENCE: Roast tools are commoditized (many clones) → low moat; differentiation must come from artifact quality or audience niche.

### Gaps
- No public numbers for LinkedIn Wrapped-style tools, "Which job will AI take" calculators, Truity revenue, Aragon revenue, or interview-prep tools (not searched due to budget).
- No public share-rate figures for 16Personalities or roast tools.

## Q3. Fake-door / pre-sale / WTP test methodology and thresholds

### Takeaway
There is no industry-standard "buy-click %" threshold; practitioners advise pre-registering a threshold and comparing to a category baseline. A commonly cited in-product threshold is ~5% click-through from the target audience; small samples (30–50 qualified visitors) are a stated minimum.

### Cited Findings
- FACT: in-product fake-door hypothesis example: at least 5% of relevant users click within two weeks; set threshold before launch — [koji.so guide](https://www.koji.so/docs/fake-door-testing-guide); [rocket.new](https://www.rocket.new/blog/fake-door-testing-validate-demand-before-you-build)
- FACT: author wants 30–50 qualified visitors minimum; untested price point can skew click rate up or down by ~10x; reports ~2% form completion on a failed iteration (2026-05-15) — [Preuve](https://preuve.ai/blog/fake-door-test)
- FACT: fake-door = finished-looking offer, measure buyer action (buy click, paid deposit, trial signup) — [Preuve](https://preuve.ai/blog/fake-door-test)

### Inferences
- INFERENCE: Because price swings click rate by up to 10x, a 7-day test should show a real price on the button and ideally use a real Stripe Payment Link (actual payment or refundable deposit) rather than a click-only door.
- HYPOTHESIS (thresholds for a 7-day experiment, derived from above, not industry standards): result-page → "buy $X" click ≥5% of completers = continue; actual payments ≥1% of completers at $9–19 = strong; <1% clicks after ≥100 completers = kill monetization angle.

### Gaps
- No sourced Van Westendorp quick-version thresholds found (not searched due to budget).
- No sourced data on pre-order → actual payment completion rates.

## Q4. Channels for 35–55 professionals (near-zero budget)

### Takeaway
Launch platforms give a one-time spike of hundreds to low thousands of visitors (Show HN ~500 uniques in one documented case; Product Hunt mostly 100–2,000 users). Paid LinkedIn is expensive; Telegram native ads in business/IT channels are also costly in 2025–26. Organic posting and owned audience appear to be the only near-zero-cost routes.

### Cited Findings
- FACT: one Show HN front-page postmortem: ~6,000 page views, 500+ unique visitors from HN, launch-day peak ~468 active users, ~20% bounce — [Indie Hackers HN postmortem](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/front-page-of-hn-the-full-postmortem-traffic-lessons-surprises-cbe9e0a7f6)
- FACT: Product Hunt survey: 24.5% of launches got 100–500 users, 26.5% got 500–1,000, 22.4% got 1,000–2,000, 10.2% got 2,000–5,000 — [Usertour](https://www.usertour.io/blog/product-hunt-gain)
- FACT: Tally #1 Product of the Month: 766 signups launch day, 785 next day, weekly signups +91% after — [Tally](https://blog.tally.so/product-hunt-launch-results/)
- FACT: Telegram native ads in micro/mid channels found more efficient and cheaper than large channels (study) — [ppc.world](https://ppc.world/news/razmeschat-reklamu-v-mikro-i-srednih-telegram-kanalah-effektivnee-i-deshevle-issledovanie/amp/)
- FACT: newsletter placements in career/professional niches ~$500–3,000 each — [Dupple](https://dupple.com/learn/newsletter-advertising-cost-2026)

### Inferences
- INFERENCE: HN/PH audiences skew young/technical — weak fit for 35–55 non-tech professionals; use them for raw traffic volume, not target-audience signal.
- INFERENCE: For a Russian-speaking 35–55 audience, micro Telegram channels (cheaper CPM per study) and Founder's own Threads/Telegram are the realistic first-100 route.
- HYPOTHESIS: a single LinkedIn post by the Founder with a tool link, plus personal DMs, can reach first 100 users; needs measurement via #src tags.

### Gaps
- Reddit community self-promotion rules, LinkedIn organic reach numbers for tool posts, YouTube comments, and "learning in public" effect: not researched (budget). No sourced numbers.

## Q5. Why such micro-tools die

### Takeaway
Documented failure drivers: high churn in AI apps, novelty/tourist users inflating early metrics, viral loops that expire without retention, and commoditization by clones.

### Cited Findings
- FACT: AI apps annual retention 21.1% vs 30.7% non-AI; monthly 12-month 6.1% vs 9.5% — [PPC Land / RevenueCat 2026](https://ppc.land/ai-apps-earn-41-more-per-user-but-churn-30-faster-revenuecat-finds/)
- FACT: a16z: early months contain tourists; measure from M3 — [a16z](https://a16z.com/ai-retention-benchmarks/)
- FACT: high-virality/low-retention growth "almost always unsustainable" as loops expire — [Agile Brand Guide citing Andrew Chen](https://agilebrandguide.com/wiki/metrics/virality-coefficient-k-factor/)
- FACT: apps launched 2025+ account for only 3% of subscription revenue; new launches rose from ~2,000/month (Jan 2022) to 14,700/month (Jan 2026) — [PPC Land / RevenueCat 2026](https://ppc.land/ai-apps-earn-41-more-per-user-but-churn-30-faster-revenuecat-finds/)
- FACT: many LinkedIn-roast clones exist — [creati.ai](https://creati.ai/ai-tools/roastmylinked-in/)

### Inferences
- INFERENCE: A 7-day experiment cannot measure retention meaningfully; kill criteria should focus on completion, share and payment intent, not D30.
- INFERENCE: ChatGPT substitution risk is highest when the output is plain text the user could prompt themselves; a designed visual artifact or a proprietary scoring frame reduces it.

### Gaps
- No sourced study specifically on "ChatGPT substitution" killing micro-tools.

# Global $0 distribution for ШАР: research (GROWTH-DIST-GLOBAL-001, 2026-10-04)

**Question.** How does ШАР (`/lab/a/`, the КОМПАС reveal) get its first 100–1000 English-speaking players, and their feedback, for $0? Should we translate now?

**North star** (reset §20): *does the toy reproduce?* Raw traffic is only useful if it gives us `ref_*` events: a player shares, and a new player arrives through that share.

## 0. Verdict on translation

**Translate now. Hold the big launch.**

- **Why translate now.** Every English channel below needs a game that a stranger can play in English. A Russian-only link is dead on arrival, and some channels (r/WebGames and r/playmygame norms) would remove it. The agents need 1–2 hours, with no human time, and the change can be reversed.
- **What to hold back.** Show HN is effectively one-shot, so wait to post it until the reveal has passed a small sample (§4). The reveal failed on meaning in round 1 (DECISION #020). A second failure in front of 10k HN readers would waste our best channel.
- **What "translate" covers.** Translate as an **adaptation** of the whole surface, not only the screen text:
  - the 8 item labels, the title words (8 adjectives + 8 nouns) and the contradiction lines;
  - the share text and the `/r/<code>/` result pages, with their English OG previews;
  - the friend-guess screen.
- **Adaptation notes.**
  - «ЗАГРАНПАСПОРТ» becomes "PASSPORT".
  - "Someone else's cat" keeps its joke.
  - The «ПРЫГАЮ Я» line must stay funny ("I JUMP").
  - An EN native-speaker read is the only human-ish risk. The Founder can do a 2-minute taste pass.

## 2. What made comparable toys spread (mechanics only)

- **Wordle grid.** The result is shared **without the spoiler**: it shows the shape of your play, not the answer. A colored pattern creates "what's yours?". [CUNY](https://pressbooks.cuny.edu/inspire/part/the-wordle-effect-how-five-letters-changed-the-internet/) For us, the story card should show the *pattern* (CARE > HOME > FREEDOM, the item thrown first) but not explain it.
- **neal.fun (Spent, Deep Sea).** "Asks you to do something small immediately", with no intro screen. [webiano](https://webiano.digital/neal-fun-is-the-web-that-still-rewards-curiosity/) ШАР already does this (the first tap is the first throw).
- **Absurd Trolley Problems.** After each choice you see **what % of the internet chose the same**. [FlowingData](https://flowingdata.com/2022/07/06/absurd-trolly-problems/) This is the cheapest upgrade we have. Our public `kept_<code>` abacus counters can feed a line like "Only 9% kept the cat" with no backend. The line raises identity ("I'm rare") and share intent.
- **16personalities and "Which X are you".** A short **code plus a name** becomes an identity object people put in bios, which is what reset §10 asks for.
- **Would You Rather sites.** A binary forced choice plus a live split. The fuse in ШАР is the same tension in game form.
- **Common denominator.** Under a minute, no signup, a result that is *about the player*, and a visible comparison to others or a friend. ШАР has all of these except the comparison to others.

## 3. The "AI agents run this company" angle

- **Where it gets attention.** HN when it has substance; Indie Hackers; the X/Bluesky AI-builder crowd; AI newsletters (they pick from HN and X, not from cold pitches).
- **Proof that Project Vend works.** Anthropic's experiment, where Claude ran a shop, spread *because of its documented failures* (tungsten cubes sold at a loss, an identity crisis). [Anthropic](https://anthropic.com/research/project-vend-1), [the-decoder](https://the-decoder.com/anthropics-claude-ran-a-store-and-lost-money-by-selling-below-cost-and-giving-discounts/) Honest failure with numbers is the story.
- **Counter-example: Auto-Co.** "14 agents run a startup" on Show HN got **4 points**, and the one commenter asked "there are so many of these… what's the difference?". [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47281538) A framework or claim with no outcome does not land.
- **What makes us credible, in this order:**
  1. a public repo with **DECISIONS.md** (reversals like #020, where the Founder overrode QA's guess in #021), **LEDGER.md** ($0), **FOUNDER_TIME.md**, **HUMAN_REQUESTS.md**;
  2. real funnel numbers, bad ones included (Genesis §50: "never manipulate metrics");
  3. a playable artifact.
- **Gap.** The repo description is stale ("a simulator for lives you haven't lived yet"), there are 0 stars, and there is no top-level "start here" for outsiders. An HN reader will look for one page explaining *who decides what*.
- **The hook as one line:** "A human gives taste; agents kill their own prototypes. Here's the decision log and the toy that survived."

## 4. Ranked plan: top 7

Ranking = (expected players × feedback quality) ÷ human effort.

**1. EN adaptation plus channel tagging (agent-only, about 2 h).**
- Translate everything listed in §0, using a separate `pid` (e.g. `a_en`) so EN counters don't mix with RU.
- Add `?src=<channel>` → `track('src_'+src)`. Abacus can't see referrers, so without this we can't tell HN from Reddit.
- Add the "X% kept the same item" line from public `kept_*` counts.
- Add an English README "start here" to the repo, with the AI disclosure.

**2. Founder's own English-speaking network, 10–20 people (Founder, about 10 min).**
- Same protocol as HR-005: send 1:1 and observe.
- This gives the best-quality feedback and the first real `ref_*` chain in English.

**3. r/playmygame post (Founder's own Reddit account, about 20 min plus replies).**
- Title like "A 1-minute balloon game: throw your life overboard, see your value compass (made by an AI-agent team, human-tested)".
- Flair Web; ask 2 concrete questions (where did you hesitate; did the compass feel like you).
- One post per month maximum.

**4. Owned build-in-public account on X and Bluesky (Founder creates it once; agents draft daily; human posts in about 2 min per day).**
- Format: Genesis §50 "BORN WEIRD // DAY N", with real counters and the day's decision.
- Expect a slow start. Its value is compounding proof for #5.

**5. Show HN (Founder writes it himself, then about 3 h live in comments).**
- **Gate, all three required:**
  - the EN sample shows completion of at least about 60%;
  - at least one `ref_start` from a non-Founder share;
  - there are at least 7 days of public log.
- Frame it as the company experiment with the toy as the try-it link and the repo as proof.
- No upvote asks. Use an aged account. Post Tue–Thu morning US time.
- This is the only action in the 1000+ range.

**6. Indie Hackers post (Founder or owned account, about 30 min).** Two weeks in, publish "What an AI-agent org learned testing a toy on N humans", with the funnel and the reversals. It recycles #4 and #5 for builders.

**7. itch.io page plus a Useless Web submission (Founder: one account and one form, about 20 min; agents prepare assets).** A cheap long tail, with GenAI disclosure ticked.

**Later or skip:**
- **TikTok/Shorts:** do it once the reveal is proven; a 12-second "what would you keep?" clip.
- **r/WebGames:** only from an account with history.
- **Skip:** Product Hunt, r/InternetIsBeautiful (banned), Newgrounds.

### Who does what

- **Agents only:** EN build, `src` tags, the "% kept" line, README, post *facts and drafts* (not for HN), daily log drafts, metric read-outs.
- **Founder, one time:** create the owned X/Bluesky account (and itch.io if used), approve the AI-disclosure wording, do a 2-minute EN taste pass.
- **Founder, per post:** Reddit and HN from his personal aged accounts. HN text must be his own words. Reply to comments personally.
- **Never:** agent-sent DMs, alt accounts, upvote requests, or hiding that the game is AI-built.

### What our counters can and cannot tell us (`product/site/lab/lab.js`)

- **Per load** we get `view`, `start`, `done`, `share_click`, `share_ok`, `kept_<code>`, `contra_*`, `rec_*`, `jump`/`drop`. A visitor who came through a shared `#ref=` link also fires `ref_view`/`ref_start`/`ref_done`. The Founder is excluded with an `f_` prefix.
- **Channel funnel:** `src_<ch>` → `view` → `start` → `done` → `share_ok`. The first step needs action 1.
- **Reproduction signal:** `ref_start ÷ share_ok`, which approximates recipients who actually play, and `ref_done ÷ done`.
  - "Organic view" = `view − ref_view` = channel traffic.
  - `ref_view` = the toy reproducing. Only this answers the north-star question.
- **Limits:**
  - counts are per page load, not per unique person, and are public and spoofable;
  - there are no referrer or channel splits without `src`;
  - samples under about 30 `done` per channel are anecdotes. The reset itself says not to overfit tiny samples.

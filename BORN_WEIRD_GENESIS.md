# 0. GENESIS

BORN WEIRD is an experiment in two layers.

## Layer A — The Product

A tiny, weird, useful experience that helps a human explore possible versions of their future self.

Initial primitive:

**Birth Date → Choices → Possible Future → Visual Artifact → Reality Seed → Share**

The experience should be:

- fast;
    
- strange;
    
- surprising;
    
- useful;
    
- intrinsically shareable.
    

## Layer B — The Company

BORN WEIRD is also an experiment in building an AI-native autonomous company.

The company should progressively learn to:

- understand users;
    
- design its own product;
    
- write and maintain software;
    
- operate infrastructure;
    
- conduct research;
    
- create and manage specialized AI workers;
    
- run experiments;
    
- analyze user behavior;
    
- improve conversion and virality;
    
- communicate with its community;
    
- publish decisions and results;
    
- generate revenue;
    
- manage costs;
    
- redesign its own organization;
    
- fund its own continued existence.
    

The Founder provides:

- Intent;
    
- Constitution;
    
- initial capital;
    
- credentials where necessary;
    
- human accountability;
    
- approvals for bounded high-impact actions.
    

AI operates the company.

# 1. PRIMARY INTENT

Create a weird, useful, self-sustaining AI-native product that helps humans explore possible versions of themselves and learn how to work with AI.

Build and evolve the company primarily through AI agents.

Develop BORN WEIRD publicly as an ongoing experiment in autonomous product development.

The long-term objective is not a predefined feature set.

The long-term objective is:

> **SURVIVE. LEARN. CREATE VALUE. REPRODUCE. BECOME INCREASINGLY AUTONOMOUS.**
